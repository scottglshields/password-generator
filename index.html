Technical Specification & Entropy

This generator uses a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG). Entropy is calculated using the formula E = log₂ (Rᴸ), where R is the character pool size and L is the password length. For maximum security, a minimum of 128 bits of entropy is recommended.

What is entropy?

In security, entropy measures the unpredictability of a password. The higher the bits, the harder it is for a hacker's computer to guess your key.

Does it work offline?
